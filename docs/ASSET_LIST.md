# Asset List — AR Industrial Safety Training

Tracks 3D models, textures, and other assets used in the AR training modules, along with their poly/texture budgets for low- and mid-range Android devices.

**Status:** Template — entries to be filled in as assets are sourced/created and budgets are confirmed with Person 1 (Unity/device profiling).

## Budget guidelines (draft — confirm with Person 1 before locking in)

| Tier | Poly count (per model) | Texture resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Hero / interactive props (e.g. extinguisher, PPE items) | 1,500–3,000 tris | 512×512 or 1024×1024 |
| Background / environment props | 500–1,500 tris | 256×256 or 512×512 |
| Simple UI/markers/icons | under 500 tris | 256×256 |

These are starting suggestions for AR on low/mid-range Android — adjust once real device testing (Person 4, Day 5–6) shows actual performance headroom.
